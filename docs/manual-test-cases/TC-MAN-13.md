**Type:** Positive / Regression
**Priority:** Medium

nopCommerce is running at http://localhost:8081/

## Test Steps
1. Open the homepage.
2. Navigate to Computers.
3. Open the Notebooks category.
4. Find the Sort by dropdown menu.
5. Select Price: Low to High.
6. Verify that products are sorted by ascending price.

Products are displayed in ascending price order.
The cheapest product appears first.
No errors are displayed.
